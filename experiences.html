Experiences.

The Philosophy

"Most of our experiences happen without being asked for — tea service on the verandah, the fire lit at six-thirty, mist rising over the tea at six. Others you arrange. All of them move at the pace of the estate."

The Signature Experience

The Fire Is Lit at Six-Thirty.

In the Planter's Lounge, at exactly six-thirty every evening, the original 1890 stone fireplace is lit. Leather armchairs worn soft by a century of use. A pot of estate tea. Ceylon arrack if you prefer. The mist settling outside. Conversations drift.

There is nothing to book. No menu. No timed session. The fire is simply there — for you, every evening, for as long as you want to sit by it.

When
From 6:30 PM, every evening
Where
The Planter's Lounge · By the original hearth
Complimentary for all guests No booking needed

An Unhurried Day

The Rhythm of the Estate

Experiences don't queue or compete — they simply unfold across the day. Here's what a typical one looks like, though yours will move to your own pace.

6:00–8:30 AM
Morning Tea & Breakfast
Mist lifts over the tea fields. Tea poured in the Morning Room. Breakfast at the Long Table.
Morning
The Day Begins
Estate walk · trail hike with packed lunch · reading on the verandah · swimming in the pool.
3:30 PM
Planter's Afternoon Tea
Three-tier tea service on the verandah. Estate-fresh Ceylon tea. Small savouries and sweets.
6:00 PM
Sundowner & Fire
Garden drinks before dinner. Fire lit at six-thirty in the Planter's Lounge. Heritage talk if you like.
7:30 PM
Dinner at the Pavilion
Lantern-lit dinner under the Tea Pavilion. Long, slow, cooked in the open. Multiple courses in their own time.
9:00 PM
Fireside & Snooker
The Planter's Lounge. A game in the Billiards Room. Ceylon arrack. Mist heavy outside.
Bedtime
The Estate Sleeps
Your chamber waiting. Fire crackling down. The hill country silent.
Repeat
— Slowly —
Most guests stay 2–3 nights. By the second day, you stop checking the time. By the third, you stop wanting to leave.
