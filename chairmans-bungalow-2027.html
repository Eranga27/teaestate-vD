The Second Property

A Plantation Bungalow on Hantana Estate

The Chairman's Bungalow stands on Hantana Estate — the same working tea plantation where the Pekoe Trail's Stage 1 begins at the Ceylon Tea Museum. It is a colonial-era residence of considerably greater scale than the Tea Bungalow in Galaha, and carries the weight of a more senior position in the estate hierarchy.

We are currently restoring the bungalow to receive guests — attending to its original fabric with the same care taken at Galaha: preserving the proportions, the materials, and the character of a house that has stood through over a century of Ceylon Tea history.

The Chairman's Bungalow will open in 2027. It will be available as an exclusive whole-house experience, part of a growing network of heritage stays under the Old Ceylon Heritage Collection. Waitlist members will receive first access and preview rates.
