The Estate, Room by Room

From the car porch through to the fire-lit lounge — the rhythm of the house, in the order you'll discover it.

Step beneath the car porch and through the front doors. The Morning Room receives you — light through tall windows, early tea poured, the day quietly beginning. This is where every stay begins.

East light · Welcome ritual

When You Have the Whole House

A Day at the Estate

An imagined rhythm — the kind of day a buyout makes possible. Adapted entirely to your party, but here's how it might unfold.

6:30 AM
Mist on the verandah
Early tea poured in the Morning Room. The estate is still quiet — the children sleeping, the hill country slowly waking.
8:00 AM
Breakfast at the Long Table
Twelve seats. Estate-fresh tea, eggs, fresh fruit, local breads. Conversation drifts between trail plans and lazy garden mornings.
9:30 AM
The day splits
Some leave for Pekoe Trail Stage 2 — packed lunches in their daypacks. Others take a guided estate walk. The grandparents stay in the Carriage House garden with coffee and a book.
1:00 PM
Long lunch on the verandah
Cold rice and curry, fresh salads, the children back from the garden. Mist beginning to settle on the tea fields. No rush.
4:00 PM
Afternoon at the pool
The hikers return, tired and triumphant. Foot soaks on the verandah. A long swim in The Private Pool. Tea service appears unprompted.
6:30 PM
The fire is lit
The Planter's Lounge fills. Ceylon arrack, leather armchairs, the original 1890 hearth. Conversations stretch into the evening.
8:00 PM
Dinner in the Tea Pavilion
Lanterns lit. The garden quiet. A long, slow meal cooked in the open by your private chef — courses that arrive in their own time.
10:30 PM
Snooker, mist, sleep
A game in the Billiards Room. The mist heavy outside. Each guest finds their chamber — six along the verandah, two more in the cottage. The fire crackles down. The estate is silent.
