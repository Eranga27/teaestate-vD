Trail FAQ

Hiker Questions, Honestly Answered

Do I need to be experienced to hike Stages 1, 2 and 3?
+

Stage 1 (Easy) is suitable for any reasonably fit walker — gentle descents, well-marked paths, around 4 hours. Stage 2 (Difficult) requires more endurance, with elevation changes and longer time on uneven ground. Stage 3 (Moderate) is somewhere in between. We recommend Stages 1 and 3 for first-time long-distance hikers, and only attempting Stage 2 with proper hiking shoes and a reasonable fitness baseline.

Are leeches really a problem?
+

On Stage 2's forest sections, yes — particularly during or after rain. We sell leech socks (essential gear for that stage), provide salt, and brief you on how to wear them properly. Stages 1 and 3 are generally clear. Don't let the leeches put you off — they are a small, manageable part of hiking in Sri Lanka's hill country.

How does the luggage transfer work?
+

Two main options. (1) Leave your main bag at the bungalow during your stay and hike with only a daypack. (2) For the Complete Stage 1 Journey package, your luggage is moved from the Hantana Cottage to the Tea Bungalow Galaha during your Stage 1 hike — it is in your room when you arrive. For Stage 3 onwards, we can arrange transfer to your next accommodation.

Do I need a Pekoe Trail pass?
+

Yes — a digital trail pass is mandatory and supports trail maintenance. You can purchase your own from the official Pekoe Trail website, or we can arrange it on your behalf as part of your booking. Have the confirmation ready on your phone for trail patrol checks.

What's the best time of year to hike?
+

The primary dry season is December to April, with January and February the most settled. A secondary window is July to September. We hike year-round, but during heavy monsoon months (May, June, October, November) trails can be slippery and leech activity high. We always check current conditions and brief you the morning of your hike.

Can I hike with a guide?
+

Yes. Local knowledgeable guides can be arranged for any stage with 48 hours' notice — particularly recommended for Stage 2 if you want context on James Taylor and the history of Loolecondera. Solo and unguided hiking is also fine; the trail is well-marked.

What if I'm slower than the estimated times?
+

The estimates are average pace — many hikers finish faster, some slower. Our pickups are flexible. Just message us via WhatsApp 30 minutes before you reach the trail end and we will be there. We do not rush you.

Are children welcome on the trail?
+

Stage 1 is suitable for confident older children (10+) with good walking experience. Stage 2 is too demanding for most children. The estate itself is very child-friendly — large garden, pool, the Carriage House Cottage is well-suited for families. Younger children are best left at the bungalow with one parent for the harder hiking days.
